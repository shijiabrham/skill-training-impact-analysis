# Skill-training-impact-analysis
Five-year analysis of skill training and employability outcomes using Python, MySQL and Tableau.
The project assessed outcomes across training completion, placement, salary growth, employment retention and post-training survey outcomes. It also examined changes in beneficiaries' income, financial condition, confidence and ability to save through follow-up survey data.
The analysis included data cleaning, exploratory data analysis and outcome assessment. An interactive Tableau dashboard was independently developed to demonstrate how programme findings could be presented through data visualisation.

## Analysis Areas
- Training and placement outcomes
- Employment continuity and retention
- Salary progression and employment mobility
- Household socio-economic outcomes
- Financial condition and savings behaviour
- Beneficiary confidence and future earning expectations
- Integrated outcome analysis across programme and survey data
- Gender and workforce participation patterns
- Employer perspectives and future skill requirements
- Labour market and sector analysis
